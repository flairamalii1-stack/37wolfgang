01 / About

Built
Independent.

37WOLFGANG is a Nigerian record label and talent management company based in Jos, Nigeria. Founded in 2024 by artist and producer Flairr Amalii alongside Smokey, the company exists to support independent African sound, artists and production.

Operating within the AMALII WORLD ecosystem, 37WOLFGANG works across artist development, management, recording, production and creative direction.

The objective is simple: build artists, build records and build freedom.
