01 / About
Built from Jos. Built for the long run.

37WOLFGANG is an entertainment company founded by Macanthonii "Big Smoke" Onyeagba and John-Louis "Flairr" Amalii.

What began as a creative team grew into a multidimensional, family-based entertainment ecosystem with experience spanning more than a decade.

Our work sits across artist development, management, recording, production, creative direction, consultancy and culture.

We believe artists should be able to build careers on their own terms — with the freedom to experiment, evolve and create work that lasts.

02 / The Wolfgang

More than
music.

37WOLFGANG was built around the idea that art and culture could be bigger than the conventional boundaries placed around them.

05 / Current Roster
Founder / CEO / Recording Artist / Producer

Flairr
Amalii

Flairr Amalii is a Nigerian rapper, singer, songwriter, self-taught producer, audio engineer, artist manager and entrepreneur based in Jos, Nigeria.

Born John-Louis Okoh Amalii, Flairr is the founder and CEO of 37WOLFGANG and one of the principal creatives behind its development over the years.

His work moves between hip-hop, trap, Afrobeats, R&B and experimental music, while his production and creative direction extend beyond his own catalogue.

Flairr has been responsible for the early development of artists including YN Asahd, TNTDRE, Kayll Universe, Psycho Le Negra and Asufii, while continuing to develop his own catalogue and manage artists within the Wolfgang ecosystem.

He has received co-signs from M.I Abaga, recorded on M.I Abaga's "The Wolf" album and has production and management credits across the Nigerian independent music scene.

Outside music, Flairr is a licensed pharmacist and entrepreneur.
